Our Story

Born From a Love of Spice

Spicy Kitchen was born in 2004 out of a single obsession: recreating the bold, soul-warming flavours of home-cooked South Asian food. Founder Priya Sharma started with a 12-seat family kitchen and a single recipe notebook.

Today, we serve over 500 guests daily across our dine-in and delivery service — but every dish is still prepared with the same passion as day one.
