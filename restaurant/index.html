Our Story

Cooking Passion
Since 2004

Spicy Kitchen began as a single tandoor oven and a dream to bring the real flavors of Indian home cooking to New York. Over two decades, we've grown into a beloved neighborhood institution — but our heart remains the same: bold spices, generous portions, and the warmth of family dining.

✓ Over 50 authentic dishes from across India
✓ All spices blended and ground in-house daily
✓ Zero artificial flavours or preservatives
✓ Family-friendly atmosphere, private dining available
✓ Full catering service for events up to 500 guests
