Our Story

Cooked With Heart,
Served With Passion

Spicy Kitchen was born in 2004 from a single dream — to bring the bold, soulful flavors of traditional Indian cooking to the heart of New York City. What started as a small family kitchen with 12 tables has grown into a celebrated culinary landmark with a loyal community of food lovers.

Every recipe is a tribute to our roots. We honor the art of slow-cooking, hand-ground spices, and the warmth of home-style cuisine while embracing modern creativity and techniques.

✔ Over 50 authentic dishes from across India
✔ 12,000+ guests served annually
✔ Awarded "Best Indian Restaurant NYC" 4 years running
✔ 100% vegetarian-friendly options on every menu
✔ Private dining and event catering available
