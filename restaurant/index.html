Our Story

Cooking with Heart & Soul since 2004

Spicy Kitchen was born in a tiny Brooklyn apartment kitchen, where founder Riya Sharma spent her weekends recreating the bold recipes passed down from her grandmother in Rajasthan, India.

What started as Sunday dinners for family and friends quickly became a buzzing supper club, and in 2004 we opened our first restaurant. Today, Spicy Kitchen has grown into a beloved culinary destination — but the same grandmother's spirit still lives in every plate we serve.
