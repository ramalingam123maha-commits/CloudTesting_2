Our Story

A Legacy of Authentic Flavour

Spicy Kitchen was founded in 2002 with a single belief — that great food can change a person's day. Starting as a small family-run kitchen in New York, we've grown into a beloved restaurant without ever losing the soul of home-cooked Indian cooking.

✓Recipes passed down through three generations of our founding family
✓Ingredients sourced fresh from local farms and specialist spice importers
✓Every sauce, marinade, and dough prepared in-house from scratch daily
✓Proudly serving 200+ guests per weekend with warmth and consistency
✓Award-winning dining experience recognised by the NYC Food & Drink Guide
