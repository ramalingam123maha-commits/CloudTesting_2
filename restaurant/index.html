Our Story

A Legacy of Spice & Passion

Spicy Kitchen was founded in 2004 by Chef Rajan Mehta, who left his hometown in Rajasthan with nothing but a suitcase of spices and a dream to share the true flavors of India with the world. Twenty years later, we have served over 10,000 guests from every corner of the globe.

Every recipe is rooted in tradition yet elevated with modern technique. We believe food is love — and every dish we make carries that philosophy.

✔ Authentic recipes passed down through generations
✔ Award-winning cuisine recognized by NYC Food Guide
✔ Vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options available
✔ Private dining & event catering for up to 200 guests
