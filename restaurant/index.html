Our Story

Cooking with Heart Since 2004

Spicy Kitchen was born in 2004 from a simple dream — to bring the bold, soul-warming flavours of authentic Indian cuisine to the heart of New York City. What started as a tiny 20-seat restaurant has grown into a beloved dining institution, but our commitment to quality and tradition has never wavered.

✓Over 22 years of perfecting authentic North & South Indian recipes
✓Fresh ingredients sourced daily from local farms and suppliers
✓4 culinary awards including Best Indian Restaurant NYC 2022
✓In-house spice blends ground fresh every morning
✓Trusted by 9,800+ loyal guests and counting
