Our Story

More Than a Meal —
It's an Experience

Founded in 2002 by Chef Rajan Sharma, Spicy Kitchen was born from a dream: to bring the vibrant, soul-warming flavours of authentic Indian cooking to the heart of New York. Over two decades later, we're still cooking with the same passion, love, and a whole lot of spice.

✓ Over 22 years of authentic Indian culinary heritage
✓ Spices imported directly from Kerala, Rajasthan and Kashmir
✓ Multi-award winning — Best Indian Restaurant 2021, 2022 & 2023
✓ Family-friendly dining with a private events space
✓ Sustainable practices — zero food waste, eco-friendly packaging
