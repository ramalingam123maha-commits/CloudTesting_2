Our Story

A Legacy of Authentic Flavors

Founded in 2004 by Chef Arjun Sharma, Spicy Kitchen began as a humble 12-seat eatery in New York's Food Town district. Today, with over 22 years of craft, we remain true to our roots — every recipe still hand-written in Arjun's original notebook.

  • ✓ All recipes tested and perfected through 22+ years of authentic cooking tradition.
  • ✓ Spice blends sourced directly from partner farms in Rajasthan, Kerala, and Kashmir.
  • ✓ Our tandoor oven has been fired continuously since 2004 — not a single day off.
  • ✓ Certified by the National Restaurant Association for food safety and hygiene excellence.
  • ✓ Featured in Food & Wine, Bon Appétit, and The New York Times Dining section.
