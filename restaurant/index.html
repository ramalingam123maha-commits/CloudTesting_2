Our Story

The Heart Behind Spicy Kitchen

Founded in 2002 in the heart of New York City, Spicy Kitchen began as a humble family restaurant with a single vision — to share the vibrant, complex flavors of authentic Indian cuisine with the world.

Today, we're proud to be one of the most celebrated Indian restaurants in New York, serving thousands of guests every month while staying true to our roots, our recipes, and our passion.

  • ✓ Family recipes passed down through three generations of master chefs
  • ✓ All spice blends ground in-house daily for maximum freshness and aroma
  • ✓ Sustainable sourcing — supporting local farmers and ethical suppliers
  • ✓ Voted 'Best Indian Restaurant in NYC' four years running (2020–2024)
  • ✓ Private dining and catering for events of all sizes — from 2 to 500 guests
