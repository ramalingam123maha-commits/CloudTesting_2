Our Story

A Legacy of Authentic Flavour

Founded in 2002 by Chef Rajesh Kumar, Spicy Kitchen began as a tiny 12-seat restaurant on Flavor Street. Today, it's a New York institution known for its honest cooking, warm hospitality, and uncompromising commitment to authentic Indian cuisine.

✓ Recipes handed down across three generations of the Kumar family
✓ Spices sourced directly from Rajasthan, Kerala, and Punjab farms
✓ Every dish prepared fresh to order — no batch cooking, ever
✓ Featured in The New York Times, Zagat Guide, and Time Out NY
✓ Proud winner of the Best Indian Restaurant NYC award 2019–2024
