Featured Artist · Florence, Italy

Elena Marchetti

Painter · Mixed Media · Visual Poet

Born in Florence and trained at the Accademia di Belle Arti, Elena Marchetti spent her formative years absorbing the Renaissance masters before deliberately dismantling their influence in her own practice.

Her work occupies the liminal space between representation and abstraction — a place where memory, light, and material converge into something that defies easy categorisation. Each canvas is built up over weeks of layering and erasure, creating surfaces of extraordinary depth and luminosity.
